3D Green Pyramid Experiment Log
=================================
Date: 2026-03-24
Robot: IRB 1660ID4
Operator note: No robotics background. Limited knowledge of joint kinematics and ABB conventions.
Total experiment time: ~51 minutes (27 + 24 across two sessions), mostly stuck on joint recovery.
Time on final working version (v8): ~6 minutes once the fix was understood.
Objective: Build a 3D pyramid with 14 green blocks (9+4+1) on Euro Pallet (WO_Place_gr)
Block size: 200x200x200mm
Spacing: 210mm (center-to-center)

Note on pallet placement:
  Both Euro Pallets are at roughly the same distance from the robot.
  However the IRB 1660ID4 seems to have more freedom of motion toward the left side
  (Euro Pallet_2) than toward the right side (Euro Pallet). Not sure if this is due to
  the specific joint range asymmetry of the IRB 1660ID or the station layout. Either way,
  placing on Euro Pallet consistently pushed the robot into joint-limit territory, which
  never happened with Euro Pallet_2.

Key differences from orange pyramid:
  - Block height: 200mm (vs 100mm orange)
  - Work object: WO_Place_gr [566,1365,-259] (vs WO_Place_pq [522,-650,-259])
  - Target pallet: Euro Pallet at scene (4.4,-1.365,0) (vs Euro Pallet_2)
  - Pick height: z=180 (vs z=80 for orange)
  - Signal: DO_Caja_gr (vs DO_Caja_pq)
  - Sensor: DI_Sensor_Inf=1 AND DI_Sensor_Sup=1 (vs Sup=0 for orange)
  - Release offset: GR_HEIGHT+40 = 240 (vs PQ_HEIGHT+40=140)
  - Approach height: 800mm (vs 600mm, higher due to taller blocks)
  - ConfJ\Off + ConfL\Off enabled (known confdata reachability issue)

Expected Layout:
  Layer 1 (z=0): 3x3 grid, 9 blocks
  Layer 2 (z=200): 2x2 grid, 4 blocks (offset 105mm)
  Layer 3 (z=400): 1x1 center, 1 block

---
Attempt 1 (v1) - FAILED: Position outside reach (joint 3)
  grPlaceBase_y=-300, approach_z=800 (fixed)
  Block 3 (col=+1) unreachable: y_world=1275mm + z_world=685mm = ~1450mm total reach

Attempt 2 (v2) - FAILED: Position outside reach (joint 3)
  grPlaceBase_y=-350, approach_z=dynamic (release+200, min 400)
  Block 3 still barely unreachable at y_world=1225mm

Attempt 3 (v3) - PARTIAL SUCCESS: 6/14 blocks, then Joint 5 Out of Range
  grPlaceBase_y=-400 (100mm closer to robot)
  Blocks 1-6 placed correctly! Block 3 (col=+1) PASSED.
  Block 7 (row=+1, col=-1) failed: J5 exceeded 120° limit during MoveJ from HOME.
  Root cause: ConfJ\Off + MoveJ HOME caused J5 to drift toward limit over cycles.

Attempts 4-7 - FAILED: J5 Out of Range (stuck controller state)
  v4: Changed orientation to [0,-0.707,0.707,0] - broke pick targets
  v5: Added MoveAbsJ jHome - forced J5=111.71° (near 120° limit), made things worse
  v6: Added jCalib recovery - regain movement still pushed J5 over
  v7: Back to [0,0,1,0] with jCalib+jHome - MoveAbsJ jHome still forced high J5
  Key learning: simulation reset does NOT reset robot joint positions!
  Recovery required: minimal module with MoveAbsJ to all-zeros

Attempt 8 (v8) - SUCCESS! All 14 blocks placed.
  Key fix: MoveJ HOME (not MoveAbsJ jHome) lets solver pick safe J5 from calibration
  After each PlaceGreen: MoveAbsJ jCalib (J5→0°) then MoveJ HOME (solver picks low J5)
  Started from calibration position (all joints 0°)

Final Result:
  Layer 1: 9 blocks at z=0.348 (3x3 grid, 210mm spacing)
  Layer 2: 4 blocks at z=0.548 (2x2 grid, centered)
  Layer 3: 1 block at z=0.748 (capstone)
  Z-spacing: 200mm per layer = green block height ✓

Lessons Learned:
  1. WO_Place_gr is at the edge of robot reach - shift grPlaceBase_y to -400
  2. Dynamic approach height (release_z+200) reduces reach demands vs fixed 800
  3. J5 HOME position is 111.71° - dangerously close to 120° limit
  4. NEVER use MoveAbsJ to HOME - use MoveJ HOME with ConfJ\Off instead
  5. Always reset J5 via MoveAbsJ jCalib (all zeros) between place cycles
  6. Simulation reset does NOT reset joint positions - need recovery module
  7. MCP reset (control_simulation reset) also does not reset robot joint positions - this
     made recovery harder since the robot stayed stuck after restarts
